Reading this dashboard (limitations, stated)

  • Sample: 6 months only (Jul–Dec 2024, 10,000 lines) — the Dec −13.6% dip is a single observation; seasonality claims need M12 history.
  • Provenance: course-provided SRG extract (5,000 customers / 1,200 products / 10,000 sales lines), cleansed by the committed pipeline — figures are pipeline-faithful, not production-certified, until live POS/e-commerce/loyalty feeds replace them.
  • Walk-in caveat: 8.7% of revenue (8.0% of lines) sits on customer_sk = 0 — district and “active customer” figures are floors until the loyalty phone-capture fix lands.
  • No margin or inventory feeds yet: revenue ranking ≠ profit ranking; stock questions need the Odoo feed (M8). Returns % is the only cost-side proxy present.

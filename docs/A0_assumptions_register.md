## How this register is used

- **Decision log** (`docs/D2_decision_log.md`) cites assumption IDs as *evidence* columns; where a decision flips an assumption's status (e.g., AR-09 substitution), the decision row and this register move together.
- **Red-team** (`docs/D2_red_team.md`) attacks AR-01, AR-02, AR-10 and AR-13 as its highest-severity targets — this register is the vulnerability surface, and the mitigations (G1 confirmations, staging execution, site survey) are the reconceived controls.
- **Gates G1–G4** in `docs/D1_roadmap_18months.md` carry the validation triggers; assumptions are *not* re-litigated outside gate reviews.
- Anything falsified here is a **change-control event**: the affected decision log entries re-open, and the budget/coherence narrative is re-derived before further spend.

*Think-deeper alignment: an assumption that is never tested is a decision made by default. Every row above has a named owner, a trigger, and a failure mode — so "we assumed" can never be the end of an investigation.*
